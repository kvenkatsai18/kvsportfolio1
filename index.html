Overview

About SURFACE 2026

The SURFACE 2026 — Symposium on Research & Future Applications of Surface Modifications is a premier two-day academic event dedicated to advancing the science and engineering of surface modification technologies.

Organized by the Institute of Centre for Lipid Science and Green Propulsion (CLSP) at the University of Hyderabad, this symposium brings together leading researchers, academicians, scientists, and industry professionals to discuss the latest developments in surface modification across materials science, physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering.

As a first-of-its-kind symposium focused entirely on surface modification, SURFACE 2026 aims to bridge the gap between fundamental research and real-world applications — spanning aerospace materials, biomedical implants, energy systems, corrosion protection, and smart functional coatings.

Program

Thematic Sessions

Four focused tracks covering the breadth of surface modification science and engineering

Track 1: Surface Engineering in Aerospace and Automotive

  • Thermal barrier coatings
  • Oxidation and corrosion-resistant coatings
  • Surface texturing for drag reduction
  • Functional coatings for extreme environments
  • Additive manufacturing surface modifications

Track 2: Surface Chemistry and Nanomaterials

  • Self-assembled monolayers
  • Nanostructured coatings and nanocomposites
  • Plasma and ion-beam surface modifications
  • Green and sustainable surface chemistry
  • Molecular dynamics simulations

Track 3: Bio-Surface Modifications and Medical Devices

  • Biomaterial surface engineering
  • Antimicrobial and antibacterial coatings
  • Implant surface modifications
  • Bio-functionalization of materials
  • Drug delivery and biosensing surfaces

Track 4: Smart Surfaces and Functional Coatings

  • Superhydrophobic and oleophobic surfaces
  • Self-healing coatings
  • Stimuli-responsive surfaces
  • Energy harvesting and piezoelectric surfaces
  • Advanced characterization techniques
