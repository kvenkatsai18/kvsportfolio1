About Me

I'm an F-1 international student founder and creator of OurBuddy — a privacy-first community platform for short-distance carpooling and accommodation connections.

As the solo founder and developer, I built the entire MVP from scratch using Flutter and Firebase, launched it on the App Store in early 2025, and am actively marketing and iterating based on user feedback. The app features 24-hour auto-deleting chats, personal information masking, location-based SOS alerts, and in-app moderation — prioritizing trust, safety, and community.

Currently serving as Web Design & Developer Specialist at MoneyBot LLC, while mentoring at Startup Weekend Louisville and contributing to the broader startup ecosystem.

Publications & Research

The Integration of AI into UI and UX Design

October 29, 2025

📄 Published on SSRN

This study examines how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can be used to improve User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX) design for increased usability, engagement, and trust. The research identifies four recurring problems—limited transparency, algorithmic bias, adoption costs, and capability gaps—and reviews ways to mitigate them through adaptive interfaces, emotion-sensitive interaction, and AI-supported prototyping.

Key Focus Areas:
  • AI in design workflows
  • Explainability & Privacy in AI
  • Fairness & Inclusivity
  • People-centered design
